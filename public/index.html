What I do

Five jobs, all on contract. Most start with a look at what is there, because the right fix is rarely the one anyone first guessed.

02 / Inspection

03 / AI audit

AI audit and adoption plan

For a company with an AI mandate and no plan, or AI already in use and no rules.

  • Inventory where AI is used, what it costs, and what is governed.
  • A phased adoption plan with compliance boundaries drawn in.
  • The policy: who owns AI-assisted work, and who answers when it goes wrong.

ProofCo-led a company-wide rollout of AI-assisted development: ran the pilots, wrote the evaluation reports, and wrote the AI usage policy and engineering process policies. Authored a phased agent adoption plan for a SOC 2-bound company.

04 / Evaluation

For a leader who needs a straight read on a team, or on a person, before a big decision.

ProofLed up to 13 engineers through quarterly reviews and performance interventions, made promotion calls, and made hiring decisions. Owned staffing and capacity against the contract roadmap.

Rescue

The invoice that nearly doubled

A hand-off pushed a client's invoice to roughly double the original bid. I found the cause, corrected the engineer's practice, set up billing controls, and negotiated the resolution.

The situation

The client had a small budget and was billed ticket by ticket. An engineer who took over another engineer's work ran well over the original bid. The invoice came in at roughly twice what we had quoted, and the client was shocked.

What I did

I started by finding out why, before anyone argued about money. The cause was how the engineer who took over was working, so I corrected that practice. Then I set up billing controls for that client so a surprise like that could not happen again. Last, I sat down with the client and their project manager, negotiated a reduced invoice, and walked them through the reasoning behind the part that stayed.

How it ended

The client's trust came back, and it did not happen again. They went on to do two more feature cycles and later signed a quarter-long engagement.
